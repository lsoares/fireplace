What it keeps

Nothing. It is a set of files with no server of its own: no accounts, no database, no cookies, no analytics and no ads. The groups you choose are in the address of your addon, which is yours.

What it asks of others

Nothing either: the lists of live videos are in the addon, and the videos are played by YouTube's own player, inside Stremio, which is YouTube's to say what it keeps.
